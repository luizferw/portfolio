# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are decision-makers and technical leads at Brazilian digital agencies, startups, SaaS companies, and companies with established internal systems. They need a dependable external developer to clear a specific backlog item without committing to a permanent hire.

## Product Purpose

Luiz Witt offers on-demand software development for existing products: bug fixes, small features, APIs and integrations, and WordPress/PHP maintenance. Success is a qualified technical request that becomes a clearly scoped proposal before work begins.

## Positioning

The service turns an uncertain technical request into a traceable, finite delivery with defined scope, deadline, price, acceptance criteria, and exclusions before implementation starts.

## Operating Context

Requests are evaluated asynchronously from a problem description, expected outcome, technology, deadlines, and supporting context such as screenshots, error logs, repository access, or a staging environment. Delivery should happen in an isolated branch and pull request whenever the client project allows it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Works primarily with React, Node.js, Vue.js, PHP, and WordPress.
- Offers one-off tasks from R$ 750, short sprints, and recurring support after an initial delivery.
- The initial contact flow must direct requests to WhatsApp.
- The static Astro build and existing deployment workflow must remain compatible.
- Social profiles, email address, and WhatsApp number have not yet been supplied; use clearly isolated configuration placeholders rather than inventing them.

## Brand Commitments

Use direct, precise Brazilian Portuguese. Do not manufacture client logos, testimonials, benchmarks, availability guarantees, or emergency-support claims.

## Evidence on Hand

The approved service description, process, prices, technologies, frequently asked questions, and form fields are supplied in the project brief. No customer stories, portraits, logo assets, or metrics are available.

## Product Principles

- Scope before execution.
- Plain, objective communication.
- Integrate respectfully into the existing codebase.
- Make delivery reviewable and traceable.
- Let clients start with one well-bounded task.
